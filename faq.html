Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FloodWatch

General

What is FloodWatch?

FloodWatch is a community-based flood monitoring and early warning system that combines a mobile app for instant citizen reporting with a web dashboard for real-time visualization, alerts, and data analysis. Our goal is to help communities in flood-prone areas prepare faster and reduce disaster risks.

Who is FloodWatch for?

Citizens & Residents: Report floods and stay informed

Barangay Officials: Verify reports and manage local alerts

LGUs & Emergency Teams: Make faster disaster decisions

Researchers: Study flood patterns and climate data

Is FloodWatch free to use?

Yes! FloodWatch is completely free for communities, barangays, LGUs, and individual citizens. No subscription required. We believe flood safety should be accessible to everyone.

Website Features

What can I do with the website?

Submit Instant Reports: Auto-detect location, upload photos, describe situation

View Flood Maps: See real-time flood zones and sensor locations on interactive map

Get Alerts: Notifications for nearby flood events and warnings

Check Live Dashboard: Monitor water levels, rainfall, and risk index

Use Offline Mode: Reports saved locally and sync when you reconnect

How do I submit a flood report?

Step 1: Click "Submit Report" on the community reports section

Step 2: Enter your location (or use GPS auto-detect)

Step 3: Select severity level (Low/Medium/High)

Step 4: Describe what you see and optionally upload photos

Step 5: Click "Broadcast to the Network" — your report instantly alerts neighbors and authorities

Reports are verified within 60 seconds by volunteers or admins

How are reports verified?

To prevent misinformation, each report goes through a verification process:

  • Photo validation (AI checks for flood indicators)
  • Barangay verifiers confirm on-the-ground accuracy
  • Cross-check with nearby sensors and weather data

Spam or false reports may result in account suspension

Does FloodWatch work offline?

Yes! The app works offline. You can submit reports, and they'll automatically sync when you regain internet connection. This is crucial for areas with unstable signal during disasters.

Dashboard Details

What information is shown on the Live Dashboard?

Water Levels: Real-time water level measurements at Banago River

Flood Risk Index: AI-calculated risk level (Low/Medium/High/Critical)

Active Monitors: Number of community sensors actively reporting

24-Hour Rainfall: Total rainfall accumulation with hourly breakdown

AI Predictions: Forecast of flood peak and affected areas

How often is the dashboard updated?

Every report submitted appears on the dashboard instantly. Sensor data (water levels, rainfall) updates every 1-2 minutes. The dashboard uses real-time AJAX polling to keep your view fresh without needing to refresh the page.

Can I access the dashboard on my phone?

Yes! The website is fully mobile-responsive. Emergency responders can check live data, approve reports, and send alerts from any device. You can even install it like an app on your home screen by selecting "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" in your mobile browser.

Privacy & Safety

Is my personal data safe?

✅ HTTPS Encryption: All data is encrypted during transmission with SSL/TLS

✅ Privacy Compliance: We comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act

✅ No Selling: Your data is never sold to third parties

✅ Secure Passwords: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text

✅ Regular Audits: Security reviews conducted regularly

What data does FloodWatch collect?

Essential: Name, email, phone number, location

For Reports: GPS coordinates, flood photos, descriptions, timestamps

For Improvement: App usage patterns (no personal browsing data)

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Will my location be visible to everyone?

No. Your personal location data is kept private. When you submit a report, only the barangay/street level location is shown publicly, not your exact address. Authorities can see precise coordinates when verifying reports.

Technical & Future

What technology powers FloodWatch?

Backend: PHP with MySQL database for reliability and scalability

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript with Tailwind CSS for modern design

Mapping: Leaflet.js with CartoDB tiles for accurate visualization

Real-time Updates: AJAX polling for live data synchronization

Hosting: Can run on any PHP-compatible web server (Apache, Nginx)

Can FloodWatch integrate with government sensors or PAGASA data?

Yes! We're working with PAGASA to integrate official rainfall and typhoon forecasts. We also support IoT water-level sensors (Arduino/ESP32) for real-time hydrological monitoring. This hybrid approach combines community crowdsourced reports with scientific data for better accuracy.

Troubleshooting

The website won't load. What do I do?

Step 1: Check your internet connection

Step 2: Refresh the page (press F5 or Ctrl+R)

Step 3: Clear browser cache and cookies (Settings > Clear Browsing Data)

Step 4: Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)

Step 5: Check if the server is running (contact your system administrator)

Still having issues? Email support@floodwatch.local

My location isn't detected accurately

Solutions:

  • Enable location permission for your browser
  • Ensure you have GPS/location services enabled on your device
  • Wait 10-15 seconds for GPS to lock (in open areas, not indoors)
  • Manually adjust the location pin on the map if needed
  • Type your street address manually as an alternative

Photos won't upload. What's wrong?

Common Causes:

  • Weak internet connection — wait for stronger signal
  • File too large — photos should be under 10MB
  • Wrong format — use JPG, PNG, or GIF
  • Server storage full — contact administrator

You can also submit reports without photos; they're optional.
